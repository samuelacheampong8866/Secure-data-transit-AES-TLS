# Enhancing Data Security in Transit - AES in HTTPS and TLS

## A Comprehensive Analysis of Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) Implementation in HTTPS and TLS Protocols

University of Cape Coast - BSc Computer Science
Final Year Project Work | August 2024
Department of Computer Science and Information Technology, School of Physical Sciences

### Overview
In the digital age, securing data in transit has become increasingly critical. This research provides a comprehensive analysis of AES implementation within HTTPS and TLS protocols, examining its role in ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of data during transmission. It analyzes AES modes of operation, key management, integration within HTTPS/TLS and the impact of quantum computing on future viability

### Abstract Highlights
- Mechanisms of AES in securing data in transit
- Analysis of AES modes, key management, and integration in HTTPS/TLS
- Strengths and vulnerabilities of current implementations
- Best practices for optimizing AES to mitigate emerging threats

### Key Areas Covered
- AES-128 and AES-256 Encryption Principles
- HTTPS Secure Communication Flow
- TLS Handshake and Session Management
- Cipher Block Chaining (CBC), GCM, CCM Modes
- Performance, Efficiency and Security Analysis
- Quantum Computing Threat Assessment
